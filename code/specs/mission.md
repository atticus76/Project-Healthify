# Healthify — Project Mission

## What We Do

Healthify is a web-based health and fitness monitoring application that connects to wearable devices and collects real-time telemetry data through their APIs. It processes data such as heart rate, activity levels, and sleep patterns to help users track their health metrics, understand their progress, and stay informed about changes in their bodies.

Through a centralized dashboard, users can view their health data, set personal goals, receive reminders, and get alerts when their telemetry indicates potentially abnormal readings. The system stores user profiles, health metrics, and alert history so users can review their information and track changes over time. By integrating with compatible wearable devices and health platforms, Healthify brings these services together in one place to make health and fitness tracking more accessible and useful.

## Who We Serve - Target Audience

Healthify primarily targets beginner and intermediate athletes who want to better understand their physical activity, monitor their health metrics, and track their fitness progress.

Our goal is to provide these users with an accessible monitoring system that simplifies the interpretation of wearable data without requiring advanced technical or medical knowledge. We aim to help users stay informed, maintain consistency with their fitness goals, and become more aware of changes in their physical metrics.

## What Success Looks Like

Healthify is successful when it provides a reliable and functional platform through which users can manage and monitor their health and fitness information.

This means users can successfully create accounts, connect compatible wearable devices, retrieve and process telemetry data, and view their information through the dashboard. The system should also correctly handle its supporting functionalities, including personalized metrics, abnormality alerts, goal tracking, reminders, and progress reports.

Ultimately, success means that all components and services communicate reliably, user data is handled securely, and the application delivers a cohesive experience that helps users better understand and track their health and fitness progress.
